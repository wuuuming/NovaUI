`Image` places and rounds a picture; the pixels come from Rust as an `ImageHandle`
(`ImageHandle::rgba8(w, h, pixels)`, or decoded by `novaui::image`). Handles are interned and
shared: three elements showing one picture hold one texture.

`ImageContent { image, opacity, fit_square, hdr }`: `fit_square` shows the centre square instead of
the whole picture; `hdr` shows a gain-map JPEG's HDR rendition on an HDR display (Windows,
Direct3D 11), brighter than SDR white where the picture is. Everything else stays at SDR white.
