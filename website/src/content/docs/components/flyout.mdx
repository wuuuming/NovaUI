`widgets::Flyout` shows one host element as a flyout:

1. `reset` clears the host's rows; the app adds rows (here, components instantiated into it).
2. `open(place, anchor)` shows it and registers it as a popup.
3. After layout, `place` reads the rows and the anchor and picks the side (`Place::on(anchor,
   Side::Below, Along::Start, gap)`, flipping to the roomier side), the height and the scroll.
4. A press outside raises `Event::PressOutside`; a press on the anchor closes it rather than
   opening it again. `close` plays the exit.

The widget holds no ids and sets no sizes: the host and its rows are the sheet's.
