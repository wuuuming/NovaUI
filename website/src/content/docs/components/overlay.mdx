An overlay (desktop lyrics, a notice) is a window of its own, opened with
`platform::run_overlay` on the calling thread or `platform::spawn_overlay` on a thread of its
own. It runs an `App` like any window, with its own turn, tree and timers. It has no frame,
shadow or taskbar entry, never takes the keyboard, and stays over the other windows.

1. The app sets the tree's clear colour transparent (`Tree::set_clear`): the window shows only
   what it paints.
2. It sizes the window after layout (`Window::set_size`), here to `#content`, laid out on a
   stage that does not depend on the window's size. The window grows and shrinks about its
   bottom centre.
3. Where `nc_hit` answers `NcHit::Caption` the user drags it. `Window::place` is where it
   stands and `Event::Moved` says it moved: keep it and pass it back in `OverlayOptions::place`
   the next time.
4. `Window::set_click_through(true)` lets presses through to what is under it. The pointer
   still comes and goes over it (hover), so the plate can fade while it is over it.

Another thread talks to an overlay as to any window: by a channel, and the overlay's waker
(`Ui::waker`) handed out by the closure that makes its app.
